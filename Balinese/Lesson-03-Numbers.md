## 💬 Useful sentences

### Target language
**Umur tiang dasa warsa.**

### Pronunciation
**OO-moor tee-AHNG DAH-sah WAHR-sah.**

### Meaning
I am ten years old.

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| umur | age | OO-moor |
| tiang | I | tee-AHNG |
| dasa | ten | DAH-sah |
| warsa | year(s) | WAHR-sah |

### Target language
**Aji kuda niki?**

### Pronunciation
**AH-jee KOO-dah NEE-kee?**

### Meaning
How much is this?

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| aji | price | AH-jee |
| kuda | how much | KOO-dah |
| niki | this | NEE-kee |



## 🎲 Number games

**Number detective:** hear a number and point to it.

**Speed count:** count 1–10 slowly, then faster.

**Pretend shop:** make price tags for 5, 10 and 20.
