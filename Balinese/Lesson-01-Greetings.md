# 👋 Lesson 01 — Greetings

## 🎯 Today I can…

Say hello, thank someone, apologise politely and finish a tiny conversation.

## 🧠 New words

| English | Balinese | Easy pronunciation | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| traditional greeting | Om Swastiastu | ohm swas-tee-AHS-too | say slowly |
| thank you | suksma | SOOKS-mah | 'sooks' + 'mah' |
| thank you very much | matur suksma | MAH-toor SOOKS-mah | two parts |
| sorry / excuse me | ampura | ahm-POO-rah | 'am' + 'poo' + 'rah' |
| yes / okay | nggih | NGEEH | soft 'ng' |
| no / not | sing | SING | like 'sing' |
| good / okay | becik | beh-CHICK | 'be' + 'chick' |
| here | dini | DEE-nee | 'dee' + 'nee' |
| there | ditu | DEE-too | 'dee' + 'too' |
| how / problem | kenken | KEN-ken | two quick syllables |


## 💬 Useful sentences

### Target language
**Suksma.**

### Pronunciation
**SOOKS-mah.**

### Meaning
Thank you.

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| suksma | thank you | SOOKS-mah |

### Target language
**Matur suksma.**

### Pronunciation
**MAH-toor SOOKS-mah.**

### Meaning
Thank you very much.

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| matur | thank / respectful thanks | MAH-toor |
| suksma | thank you | SOOKS-mah |

### Target language
**Ampura.**

### Pronunciation
**ahm-POO-rah.**

### Meaning
Sorry / excuse me.

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ampura | sorry / excuse me | ahm-POO-rah |

### Target language
**Sing kenken.**

### Pronunciation
**sing ken-KEN.**

### Meaning
It's okay / no worries.

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sing | not | SING |
| kenken | problem / how | KEN-ken |



## 🗣️ Short conversation

| Speaker | Balinese | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Om Swastiastu! | ohm swas-tee-AHS-too | Hello! |
| B | Om Swastiastu! | ohm swas-tee-AHS-too | Hello! |
| A | Suksma. | SOOKS-mah | Thank you. |
| B | Nggih. | NGEEH | Yes / okay. |
| A | Ampura. | ahm-POO-rah | Sorry / excuse me. |
| B | Sing kenken. | sing ken-KEN | It's okay. |

## 💡 Grammar tip

Balinese has different speech levels. For now, learn polite forms and listen to how people around you speak.

## 🧩 Build Your Own Sentence

Choose one:
- Suksma.
- Ampura.
- Suksma + a gesture.
- Ampura + a reason.

Say it three times.

## 🎮 Fun practice

Pretend you arrive at a Balinese cultural event. Greet someone, thank them, apologise for bumping into a chair, then say goodbye.
