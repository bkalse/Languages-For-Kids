# 🙋 Lesson 02 — Introducing Yourself

## 🎯 Today I can…

Say my name, where I am from, ask someone's name and say how I feel.

## 🧠 New words

| English | Balinese | Easy pronunciation | Sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I / me | tiang | tee-AHNG | 'tee' + 'ahng' |
| you | ragane | rah-GAH-neh | 'rah-gah-neh' |
| name | wasta | WAHS-tah | 'wahs' + 'tah' |
| from | saking | SAH-king | 'sah' + 'king' |
| Australia | Australia | os-TRAH-lee-ah | like English |
| Bali | Bali | BAH-lee | 'bah' + 'lee' |
| learn | mlajah | muh-LAH-jah | 'muh' + 'lah' + 'jah' |
| Balinese | basa Bali | BAH-sah BAH-lee | 'bah-sah' + 'bah-lee' |
| happy | bagia | bah-GEE-ah | 'bah' + 'gee' + 'ah' |
| good / well | becik | beh-CHICK | 'be' + 'chick' |


## 💬 Useful sentences

### Target language
**Wastan tiange Maya.**

### Pronunciation
**WAHS-tahn tee-AHN-geh MAH-yah.**

### Meaning
My name is Maya.

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| wastan | name | WAHS-tahn |
| tiange | my / I, polite | tee-AHN-geh |
| Maya | name | MAH-yah |

### Target language
**Sira wastan ragane?**

### Pronunciation
**SEE-rah WAHS-tahn rah-GAH-neh?**

### Meaning
What is your name?

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sira | who | SEE-rah |
| wastan | name | WAHS-tahn |
| ragane | you | rah-GAH-neh |

### Target language
**Tiang saking Australia.**

### Pronunciation
**tee-AHNG SAH-king os-TRAH-lee-ah.**

### Meaning
I am from Australia.

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tiang | I | tee-AHNG |
| saking | from | SAH-king |
| Australia | Australia | os-TRAH-lee-ah |

### Target language
**Tiang kayun mlajah basa Bali.**

### Pronunciation
**tee-AHNG KAH-yoon muh-LAH-jah BAH-sah BAH-lee.**

### Meaning
I want to learn Balinese.

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tiang | I | tee-AHNG |
| kayun | want | KAH-yoon |
| mlajah | learn | muh-LAH-jah |
| basa Bali | Balinese | BAH-sah BAH-lee |

### Target language
**Tiang becik.**

### Pronunciation
**tee-AHNG beh-CHICK.**

### Meaning
I am good / well.

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tiang | I | tee-AHNG |
| becik | good | beh-CHICK |

## 💡 Grammar tip

Learn whole sentence patterns instead of translating word-for-word. Balinese pronouns and speech levels change with context.

## 🎯 Challenge

Replace **Maya** and **Australia** with your own details.
