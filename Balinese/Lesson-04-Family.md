## 💬 Useful sentences

### Target language
**Niki meme tiange.**

### Pronunciation
**NEE-kee MEH-meh tee-AHN-geh.**

### Meaning
This is my mum.

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| niki | this | NEE-kee |
| meme | mum | MEH-meh |
| tiange | my | tee-AHN-geh |

### Target language
**Tiang ngelah adi.**

### Pronunciation
**tee-AHNG ngeh-LAH AH-dee.**

### Meaning
I have a younger sibling.

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tiang | I | tee-AHNG |
| ngelah | have | ngeh-LAH |
| adi | younger sibling | AH-dee |

### Target language
**Kulawarga tiange ageng.**

### Pronunciation
**koo-lah-WAHR-gah tee-AHN-geh ah-GENG.**

### Meaning
My family is big.

### Word-by-word
| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| kulawarga | family | koo-lah-WAHR-gah |
| tiange | my | tee-AHN-geh |
| ageng | big | ah-GENG |



## 🗣️ Conversation

| Speaker | Balinese | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Sira niki? | SEE-rah NEE-kee? | Who is this? |
| B | Niki meme tiange. | NEE-kee MEH-meh tee-AHN-geh | This is my mum. |
| A | Niki adi? | NEE-kee AH-dee? | Is this your younger sibling? |
| B | Nggih. | NGEEH | Yes. |

## 💡 Grammar tip

**Ngelah** is a useful verb meaning “have”.

## 🧩 Build it

**Tiang ngelah + [family member].**

## 🎮 Fun activity

Draw your family tree and label five people.

## 🧠 Mini quiz

meme? bapa? kulawarga? ngelah?

**Answers:** mum; dad; family; have.
