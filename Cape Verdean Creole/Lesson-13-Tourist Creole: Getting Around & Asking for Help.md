## 4. Where is the beach?

**Creole:** Undi ki praya?

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| Undi | Where | OON-dee |
| ki | is | kee |
| praya | beach | PRAH-yah |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

OON-dee kee PRAH-yah?

**Meaning:**

Where is the beach?

# 🚕 Taxi

## 12. I want to go to the hotel.

**Creole:** N krê bai pa otél.

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| N | I | n |
| krê | want | kray |
| bai | go | bye |
| pa | to | pah |
| otél | hotel | oh-TEL |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

n kray bye pah oh-TEL

**Meaning:**

I want to go to the hotel.

---

## 13. Take me to the airport, please.

**Creole:** Leva-m pa aeroportu, pur favor.

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| Leva-m | Take me | LEH-vahm |
| pa | to | pah |
| aeroportu | airport | ah-eh-roh-POR-too |
| pur favor | please | poor fah-VOR |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

LEH-vahm pah ah-eh-roh-POR-too, poor fah-VOR.

**Meaning:**

Take me to the airport, please.
