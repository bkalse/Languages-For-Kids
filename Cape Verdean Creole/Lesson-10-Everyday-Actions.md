# Useful Sentences

## 1. I eat.

**Creole:** N ta kume.

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| N | I | n |
| ta | present action marker | tah |
| kume | eat | KOO-meh |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

n tah KOO-meh

**Meaning:**

I eat.

# Grammar Tip

The word **ta** is very useful.

It helps show that an action is happening or is something you do.

Examples:

- N ta kume. = I eat / I am eating.
- N ta bebe. = I drink / I am drinking.
- N ta lê. = I read / I am reading.
- N ta brinka. = I play / I am playing.

A simple pattern is:

**N ta + action**
