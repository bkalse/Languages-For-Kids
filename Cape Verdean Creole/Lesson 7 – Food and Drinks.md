# Useful Sentences

## 1. I am hungry.

**Creole:** N sta ku fome.

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| N | I | n |
| sta | am | stah |
| ku | with | koo |
| fome | hungry | FOH-meh |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

n stah koo FOH-meh

**Meaning:**

I am hungry.

---

# Grammar Tip

The word **krê** means **"want."**

You can use:

**N krê...** = I want...

Examples:

- N krê agu. = I want water.
- N krê sumu. = I want juice.
- N krê pon. = I want bread.
