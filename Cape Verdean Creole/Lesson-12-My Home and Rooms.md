# Useful Sentences

## 1. This is my house.

**Creole:** Es li nha kasa.

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| Es | This | ess |
| li | is | lee |
| nha | my | nyah |
| kasa | house | KAH-sah |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

ess lee nyah KAH-sah

**Meaning:**

This is my house.

---

## 2. This is my room.

**Creole:** Es li nha kuartu.

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

ess lee nyah KWAHR-too

**Meaning:**

This is my room.

---

## 3. I am in my room.

# Grammar Tip

You have already learned **na**, which can help say where someone or something is.

**na + place**

Examples:

- na skola = at school
- na kasa = at home
- na kuartu = in the room
- na koziña = in the kitchen
- na sala = in the living room

# Build Your Own Sentence 🧩

Use:

**[Person/thing] + sta + na + [place].**

Examples:

**Mama sta na koziña.**

Mum is in the kitchen.

**Papa sta na sala.**

Dad is in the living room.

**N sta na nha kuartu.**

I am in my room.

Try making three of your own sentences.
