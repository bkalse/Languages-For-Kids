# Useful Sentences

## 1. This is my hand.

**Creole:** Es li nha mon.

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| Es | This | ess |
| li | is | lee |
| nha | my | nyah |
| mon | hand | mohn |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

ess lee nyah mohn

**Meaning:**

This is my hand.

---

## 2. I have two eyes.

**Creole:** N ten dos ojus.

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| N | I | n |
| ten | have | ten |
| dos | two | dohs |
| ojus | eyes | OH-zhoos |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

n ten dohs OH-zhoos

**Meaning:**

I have two eyes.

---

## 6. I am wearing a shirt.

**Creole:** N sta ku un kamiza.

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| N | I | n |
| sta | am | stah |
| ku | with | koo |

| kamiza | shirt | kah-MEE-zah |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

n stah koo oon kah-MEE-zah

**Meaning:**

I am wearing a shirt.

# Conversation

## Ana

**Creole:** Ki kor ki bu kamiza?

**Pronunciation:** kee kor kee boo kah-MEE-zah?

**Meaning:** What colour is your shirt?

---

## Pedro

**Creole:** Nha kamiza e azul.

**Pronunciation:** nyah kah-MEE-zah eh ah-ZOOL.

**Meaning:** My shirt is blue.

---

## Ana

**Creole:** Bu ten sapatu?

**Pronunciation:** boo ten sah-pah-TOO?

**Meaning:** Do you have shoes?

---

## Pedro

**Creole:** Sin. N ten dos sapatu.

**Pronunciation:** seen. n ten dohs sah-pah-TOO.

**Meaning:** Yes. I have two shoes.

---

# Grammar Tip

To say **"my"**, use:

**nha**

Examples:

- nha mon = my hand
- nha kabelu = my hair
- nha kamiza = my shirt
- nha sapatu = my shoes
