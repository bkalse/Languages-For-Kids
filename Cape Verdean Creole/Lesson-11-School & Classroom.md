# Useful Sentences

## 1. I go to school.

**Creole:** N ta bai skola.

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| N | I | n |
| ta | action marker | tah |
| bai | go | bye |
| skola | school | SKOH-lah |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

n tah bye SKOH-lah

**Meaning:**

I go to school.

---

## 2. I am at school.

**Creole:** N sta na skola.

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| N | I | n |
| sta | am | stah |
| na | at / in | nah |
| skola | school | SKOH-lah |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

n stah nah SKOH-lah

**Meaning:**

I am at school.

# Grammar Tip

**Undi** means **"where."**

Examples:

- Undi ki nha lapiz? = Where is my pencil?
- Undi ki bu sta? = Where are you?
- Undi ki prufesor sta? = Where is the teacher?

# Build Your Own Sentence 🧩

Use:

**N ta + action + thing.**

Examples:

**N ta lê livru.**

I am reading a book.

**N ta skrebe papel.**

I am writing on paper.

**N ta odja kádra.**

I am looking at the board.

Try making your own sentence.
