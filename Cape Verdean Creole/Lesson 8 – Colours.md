# Useful Sentences

## 1. What colour is this?

**Creole:** Ki kor ki es li?

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| Ki | What | kee |
| kor | colour | kor |
| ki | that / which | kee |
| es | this | ess |
| li | is | lee |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

kee kor kee ess lee?

**Meaning:**

What colour is this?

---

## 2. It is red.

**Creole:** Es li korbêdju.

### Word-by-word

| Word | Meaning | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| Es | It / this | ess |
| li | is | lee |
| korbêdju | red | kor-BEH-joo |

**Sentence Pronunciation:**

ess lee kor-BEH-joo

**Meaning:**

It is red.

---

# Grammar Tip

You can use:

**[thing] e [colour]**

Examples:

- Banana e amarelu. = The banana is yellow.
- Tomati e korbêdju. = The tomato is red.
- Folha e verdji. = The leaf is green.
